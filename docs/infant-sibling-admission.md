Infant siblings have no additional Open Play admission charge through the whole sixth month, ending on the seventh-month birthday. Eligibility uses the child's saved birthday and the Eastern visit date. Month ends are clamped. A child must actually attend with a sibling; an older child merely existing on the account does not qualify. A standalone child retains the existing admission price. Infant attendance still consumes capacity and needs a waiver.

New customer and staff reservations, including previews for newly entered ages, share the same age rule. Membership coverage is applied after the free-sibling rule, so a covered older sibling also qualifies the infant.

The installed check-in RPC keeps its original waiver, occupancy, membership and prepaid protections. Its provisional unpaid fee is projected using actual same-day family attendance in the staff admission response. Paid/prepaid records are never discounted retroactively. A pending Square sale with a positive frozen infant fee retains its existing amount and recovery process; do not silently alter an already launched checkout.

At POS launch, the server includes eligible infants' underlying unpaid rows in the existing atomic full-family freeze. It claims the unlaunched sale and lowers its total in one conditional UPDATE. Only that claim's winner can zero the infant fee, append an audit note, and return a Square deep link. The zero-price row remains due until the existing receipt verifier fulfills the whole frozen sale. Display projects it as included in the interim. If writing fails after the claim, no launch URL is returned; the usual Square reconciliation/recovery process applies. No refund, receipt or previously launched sale is edited.
