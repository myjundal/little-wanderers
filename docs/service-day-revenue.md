# Private service-day revenue

The owner's personal account can open `/staff/revenue` from the owner dashboard. Both the server page and `/api/admin/revenue` require the verified owner Auth user ID **and** the operator `owner` role, with no kiosk session. Admins, other owners, staff, instructors, customers and unauthenticated visitors cannot access the report. No financial data is sent to an unauthorized browser. API responses are private/no-store, including permission failures.

The report reads current production Supabase records and retrieves their linked Square orders and completed payments, including refunds. It never invokes fulfillment/reconciliation or writes to Supabase or Square. Each source is paginated; failed/truncated sources and unmatched receipts are explicitly reported as incomplete, never treated as a verified zero. Only relevant orders for the selected service date are fetched, while allocations include all linked sessions so shared receipts conserve their amounts.

`20261111_private_revenue_party_payment_columns.sql` records the party invoice columns already verified in production, for fresh database setups. Legacy parties without a production order require review even if their quote is known: a missing payment link is not evidence that the customer owes the whole quote. This feature does not backfill or infer those payments.

Rules:

- A reservation uses its latest slot's Eastern calendar date. Walk-ins use their actual Eastern check-in date. Parties use their current start time; classes use each session's date.
- A named child's matching reservation/check-in is one service. More than one matching booking or check-in requires review. A shared order is allocated using its exact recorded service charges; the sum must match the provider's service total. Receipt IDs and amounts are validated. An unexplained mismatch is excluded from confirmed totals.
- The paid, after-discount class-series amount is allocated equally across its explicitly registered session IDs, in chronological order. Remainder cents go to earlier sessions. A canceled session keeps its original allocation for review; no silent redistribution to other dates occurs.
- General class checkouts use their selected session IDs and frozen line amounts. POS class purchases use only the production `square_catalog_mappings.variation_id` and its explicit `class_series_programs.class_ids` program link (or an unambiguous single-session mapping). A reused weekly variation without a selected date is insufficient. Names and equal prices never establish dates. These projections do not create registrations or change attendance, invoices or payments.
- Itemized Square class-order history is paginated independently of the selected day's receipts. This preserves an earlier POS prepayment when viewing a later class date, and applies new class schedule dates on refresh. Failed or capped history is explicitly incomplete. An unallocated class receipt remains visible as `unallocated_class_cents` on its payment day and is excluded from recognized revenue until its service dates are known. It must not fall back to payment-day sales.
- Class revenue is split instructor 70% / owner 30%. After service-date allocation, discounts, refunds, gift-card duplicate exclusions and receipt review, the day's recognized class total is multiplied by 30% and rounded half-up to whole cents. Exact largest-remainder allocation distributes the owner's cents across stable service IDs. `net_cents`, `class_cents`, categories, attendance totals and payment-timing components include only that owner share. `class_gross_cents`, `class_owner_cents` and `class_instructor_cents` expose the split explicitly; $120 class income contributes $36 to the owner's revenue total and $84 to the instructor share. Every row retains `gross_net_cents` and gross payment-timing fields. Original receipt/payment/refund/tax/tip amounts and customer balances remain full customer amounts; they are never written or reduced. Unallocated, cancelled and review-only class receipts contribute no recognized owner share. This applies to website drop-ins, series and catalog/POS classes, including historical reports and the daily report API.
- Deposits and later balances belong to the service date. Prepayment, same-day payment and later payment are components of that date's net received amount, not additional sales. Unpaid quotes are listed separately. Unpaid interest registrations are not sales.
- Separate POS party balance receipts may be linked through `membership_events` audit records with action `party_receipt_link`. Only the verified personal owner's records are accepted, with an explicit production order ID, booking ID, matching household, and exact service allocation in a version 1 JSON note. Original deposit orders remain linked. Live Square evidence determines paid/refunded amounts; event amounts alone never establish receipt of funds. Conflicting links fail closed, and receipt/allocation mismatches require review. Links follow the booking's current service date and are deduplicated by order ID.
- Refunds reduce the original service date, not the refund date. They do not create a new customer debt. Cancellation payments are excluded and flagged for fee/refund review. Refunds containing tax/tips and partially paid tax invoices require review because their allocation is ambiguous.
- Tax and tips are separate from service revenue. Fees are not deducted. Membership-covered admissions have no additional admission revenue. Membership/pass sales are not assigned invented daily values.
- Attendance-confirmed and attendance-unconfirmed amounts are separate. Future dates show money already received for that future service; they are not represented as completed visits. Payment dates come from provider receipts rather than booking creation or reconciliation timestamps.
- Unknown payment links, no-shows, legacy paid records without a provider order, missing allocation and invalid dates/amounts require review and are excluded from the confirmed total.

Run the same report with `node --env-file=.env.local scripts/daily-revenue.mjs 2026-11-11`. Omitting the date uses today in New York; `--summary` suppresses ordinary detail rows. The normal run uses an in-memory personal owner sign-in session to read the deployed private API, keeping the production Square credential on the server. No login email is sent and the reporting session is signed out afterward. `--local` uses the local loader directly with the explicitly configured Square environment. Exit code 2 means the output is a partial report with explicit review items; do not present it as a final total. No secret keys, customer contact details or raw provider responses are printed.

The daily thread automation runs at 8:30 PM America/New_York and uses this script and the same rules. The page refreshes from live data on each date change or Refresh. There is no nightly mutation of payment dates or accounting records. Historical totals are restated when a reservation changes date or a later refund/payment appears.

Party reporting also shows payment-day collections separately from service-day revenue. A $100 deposit today and $300 balance on the party day contribute $400 once to that party date when fully paid; today shows $100 of collections without adding it to today's revenue. A partial deposit contributes only the verified amount received, with the remaining quote shown as due. This is a management report on receipts attributed to service dates, not a formal accrual ledger or tax return.

Unlinked Square party items no longer fall through to ordinary payment-day sales. They appear as `unallocated_party_cents` on their payment day and require review. Historical order discovery includes party receipts, and a full booking UUID in order `metadata.party_booking_id` or `reference_id` (`party_<UUID>` / `party_balance_<UUID>`) can establish the current party date. Persisted booking order links take precedence. Names, prices, truncated references and invoice creation dates never establish the date. Separate explicitly linked deposit/balance orders can share one party row, and repeated discovery cannot add the same order twice. Existing bookings without payment links still require reconciliation; the report does not backfill them or send invoices.

`party_cents`, `party_prepaid_cents` and `party_due_cents` describe parties on the selected service date. `party_receipts` and `party_received_today_cents` describe payments received on the selected Eastern date, even for future parties. Collections exclude tax, tips and gift-card redemption and show gross receipts with cumulative refunds separately; refunds are not represented as happening on the original payment day. These views overlap and must never be added together.

## Clear admission totals and attendance

Admission revenue is one `admission` financial category combining linked reservations, check-ins and standalone Square admission sales. Financial categories no longer expose a `count`: a reservation, a person and an itemized receipt line are different units. The owner UI shows money only for each category. Admission's verified total is split into `linked_cents` and `unlinked_square_cents`; an unlinked settled sale remains verified money without inventing which booking/person paid it.

`visitor_stats` counts distinct production check-in person IDs on the selected Eastern date, independently of payment allocation. Selected visitors' roles are retrieved in bounded batches, and child/adult/unknown counts are explicit. Repeated check-ins count once. Adults accompanying reserved children and membership-covered guests remain attendees, without being described as walk-in sales. Reservation counts are explicitly labeled and exclude canceled bookings. Unvisited reservations are not automatically marked as no-shows.

An admission quote/check-in price with no verified payment is not evidence of collectible debt. The calculator moves its recorded unpaid amount to `pending_payment_cents`, with `payment_check` distinguishing `payment_unconfirmed` (visit recorded) from `visit_unconfirmed` (no matching visit). Neither contributes to `due_cents`, and the report remains incomplete until those records are checked. A verified partial payment on an attended admission can establish an actual remaining balance. Refunds do not reopen debt. Existing receipt/cancellation/matching issues remain separate and never imply a confirmed amount owed. Party invoice and class balances retain their existing verified-order rules.

Regression against a saved owner snapshot preserved every confirmed revenue cent while combining the two admission categories, separating reconciliation amounts from confirmed debt, and counting unique children/adults independently. No customer attendance or payment records were changed.

The personal-owner-only `GET /api/admin/revenue/admission-receipts?date=YYYY-MM-DD` supports manual reconciliation of settled Square admissions with their actual tender type, including cash and external payments. `kind=party` selects party receipts instead. It verifies production payment/order/location bindings and returns itemized amounts, provider timestamps, customer/cardholder names, card brand and last four digits, and receipt references. It omits contact details and other card fields, uses private/no-store responses, and never persists a match or creates a charge. An equal amount or nearby timestamp alone does not establish a booking link; compare the owner's confirmation and explicit customer/reference evidence before applying an audited database correction. Provider failures return an error rather than a silently incomplete candidate list.

A complimentary/covered admission that retains an existing positive-price production receipt preserves that receipt allocation and raises an explicit review issue. It contributes neither confirmed revenue nor debt. This prevents a waived visit from reappearing as a standalone Square sale after the check-in is corrected. The original quote and provider evidence remain available for review; the report does not issue a refund, delete a receipt, or claim that a provider payment was reversed.

Receipt review discovers orders both from the daily provider listing and from the selected day's persisted service/check-in links. Each settled tender is retrieved directly from its order, so an absent daily-list entry cannot hide a known POS receipt. Party review also scans itemized history for earlier deposits; returned payments retain their actual provider dates and must still be explicitly matched to the correct current booking.
