# Guest waiver family reconciliation

Public `/waiver` still saves its signed receipt atomically using the existing submission RPC. A `waitUntil` task reconciles matching guest-only families afterward, so private identity lookups do not delay the receipt. Receipt-status retries also retry linking. No database migration or new authentication grant is required.

A match to an existing account requires the same normalized guardian name, a matching contact (household phone/email or a confirmed caregiver's Auth email), and a unique compatible match for every submitted child. Accents, whitespace and US phone punctuation normalize. An existing blank child surname can match an exact first name and birthdate. A missing birthdate requires an exact full child name plus verified caregiver email or both contact fields. Conflicting birthdates, duplicate child identities, multiple candidate accounts, and unmatched children remain for review. Names alone never link accounts.

The register's contactless **New family** intake has a separate same-day rule. The target must have only anonymous staff-entered `household_members` rows matching its complete people roster, no Auth members or contact details, one matching adult and exactly the submitted children. Both households must have been created on the same New York calendar day. The guardian's surname must match either their stored surname or, when blank, the explicit family name. Each child must uniquely match first name and surname (the guardian surname may fill an absent surname), with no conflicting birthdate. Missing register birthdates are allowed in this narrow intake case. Ambiguous families remain separate. Existing account matching still requires contact evidence. Manual member rows are no longer mistaken for Auth accounts.

Signing after registration triggers the existing receipt background task. Saving **New family** after signing also reconciles recent matching guest waivers before returning to the desk. Both orders retain the register's household and person IDs, including any visits already on that family. The waiver audit records `match_basis: same_day_staff_walkin`. For this anonymous same-day intake only, missing child birthdays are completed from the original signed child snapshot and its audited person mapping, so child check-in and age-based admission pricing can proceed. Existing birthdays, source people, signed evidence, member rows and visits are preserved. Receipt retries revalidate the complete roster and can finish an interrupted birthday write; a target that has gained an Auth account or contact details is excluded. A linking error is logged without failing a successfully saved family.

Sources with account members, reservations, membership/billing records, class registrations, verified guest identities or check-ins are excluded from automatic reconciliation. Each accepted waiver keeps the same ID, signature, signing/expiry times, consent and original legal/child snapshot. Its household and covered-person IDs are mapped together to the existing family's signed children only. An additional `agreement_snapshot.family_reconciliation` audit preserves the original household, covered IDs and person mapping. Original households/people remain recoverable. Party roster links follow the same waiver ID. The existing staff directory alias is written last to suppress the duplicate search result; retries can finish an interrupted write using the saved audit. This is a staged, idempotent REST workflow, not a cross-table SQL transaction. Errors preserve the signed receipt and leave unfinished directory work reviewable.

The public response contains no matching family ID, children, account existence or linking result. Submitted contact information cannot read an account, change its members, claim its verified email identity, or grant access. Linking changes no price, payment or visit count.

The check-in desk uses one reservation/search list containing both today's booked families and walk-ins. Checked-in cards have a green background and the existing idempotent checkout action; departed cards are gray and retain arrival/departure/stay times. Other families use dark bold names. Duration statistics remain available below the workspace.

## Staff Payment confirmed

The check-in desk's Family payment panel has **Payment confirmed** for the selected family. It reads today's server-recorded admission, reconciles any existing Square POS launch using the existing provider receipt checks, and only confirms settled paid/prepaid/included admission. Clicking never creates a payment, waives an unpaid balance, starts billing or accepts a browser assertion that payment succeeded. Unverified/partial receipts remain pending; ambiguous receipts require review.

Selecting an existing reservation also searches its confirmed caregivers' email addresses for exactly one guest family with a settled visit today. It cannot claim another account or choose among multiple paid guest candidates.

For a paid guest-only family, confirmation also looks for one existing family using the existing guardian/contact/child matching rules. A target account with a verified matching caregiver email and a uniquely matching complete child roster may receive missing child surname/birthday details from the signed waiver. A missing surname is completed only when the signed child's surname matches the guardian. Conflicting birthdays, ambiguous accounts, established source accounts, unsettled historical attendance, existing same-day target attendance, unrelated source purchases/passes/invites and conflicting Square customers require owner review. Different surnames or unrelated contact details still require the owner to establish the connection; a payment alone cannot identify a family.

Accepted links preserve the existing account/person IDs and reservations. Signed waivers, guest party roster links, settled attendance and the existing Square customer mapping connect to that family. Paid amounts, order references, payment states, visit IDs/times, signatures and the original legal/child snapshots stay unchanged. Neither household nor source people are deleted, and account access stays unchanged. Reservation payment display can then find the child's recorded counter payment rather than quote it again.

A deterministic `membership_events` journal (`paid_family_reconciliation`) records the exact original rows, person mapping and staff actor before writes. Immutable snapshots are stored separately from small journal headers, so compare-and-set filter URLs stay bounded. Source and destination leases serialize retries and concurrent attempts to move different duplicates onto the same family. The staged REST workflow can resume after interrupted writes; changed evidence becomes owner review. The waiver's separate `payment_family_reconciliation` audit retains its original covered IDs. Its ordinary public reconciliation task cannot finalize this staff migration. The directory alias is written only after the visited family is fully linked, and UI refreshes both payment and reservation/family lists. Existing receipts can remain confirmed while family linking separately reports pending/review. No schema migration or scheduled job is needed.
