# Memberships purchased in Square POS

A signed completed-payment webhook retrieves the current Square order, every tender's payment and the catalog variations. `Monthly Membership - 1st Child` ($60) and the supported $20 sibling products grant one calendar month from the completed payment's Eastern date. Full settlement, USD, store location, catalog identity, one first-child item, a unique linked family customer and a compatible complete child roster are required. A matching price or an unlinked customer never grants access. Attach the family customer in Square before completing a sale; the staff family's **Connect Square customer** action creates the directory link.

Each order has a deterministic `membership_events` receipt (`pos_membership_payment`); a deterministic, finite `memberships` entitlement supplies existing family status and totals. Source receipts preserve payment IDs, paid amount, original paid date, purchased period and children. Provider reads precede each retry. Duplicate/out-of-order deliveries never add a second month, and a recorded refund cannot be changed back to paid by an older delivery. Recurring billing, saved cards, invoices, extra charges and refunds are never created.

Unpaid child admissions within the purchased period become Membership / $0. Original fees remain in the visit notes; attendance, occupancy, signatures and separately paid admission receipts are preserved. Existing launched admission sales remain in the audit; a sale whose visitors are already covered no longer locks the payment workspace. Future booking quotes and reservation labels use the paid period and original covered children, including its expiry.

Membership management shows these purchases under **Paid in person**, with Payment completed, amount, covered children and paid dates. The General and total counters include the family once. Refunded terms are inactive. An owner can replay an existing order using `POST /api/admin/founding-memberships` with `{ "mode": "sync_pos", "order_id": "..." }`; replay cannot charge a card. Preview deployments cannot synchronize Production receipts.

The existing event and membership tables are reused; no schema migration or new credentials are required. Failed provider/database/identity verification returns a retryable webhook failure. Ambiguous children, missing family links, altered products/prices and incomplete or refunded payments require review instead of guessing coverage.
